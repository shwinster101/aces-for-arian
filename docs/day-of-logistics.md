# Day-of logistics runbook — Aces for Arian

Durable, reusable checklist for running the tournament: what to **buy**
(consumables) and what to **order + confirm** (merch, trophies). The ops console
has a live, checkable version (**Logistics** tab, HQ-only) seeded from this same
list — use the tab day-of to tick things off, and update **this file** after the
event so next year's committee starts from reality.

Baseline sizing assumes ~**46 players** (≈19 doubles teams + 27 singles) plus
family/spectators, over **two days** (Sat doubles + Sun singles, 9 AM starts).
Scale up if the field grows.

---

## 1. Buy list (consumables)

Buy the day before; keep perishables cold. Restock Sunday morning from Saturday
leftovers.

| Item | Suggested qty (per year) | Got? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water (cases) | 5 cases (24-pk) | ☐ | The big one — heat + all day. Buy extra Sunday if Sat runs low. |
| Gatorade (cases) | 3 cases | ☐ | Mixed flavors; players go through these fast in the afternoon. |
| Fruit — bananas / oranges | ~4 dozen | ☐ | Easy energy between matches; oranges hold up in heat. |
| Savory snack — chips / pretzels | ~8 bags | ☐ | Pretzels/chips; individual bags travel better than one big bag. |
| Fruit snacks | 2 boxes | ☐ | Kid-friendly; good quick sugar. |
| Paper towels | 3 rolls | ☐ | Spills, sweat, cleanup. |
| Napkins | 1 pack | ☐ | For the snack table. |
| Ice + coolers | ~3 bags | ☐ | Keep water/Gatorade cold; borrow coolers ahead of time. |
| Cups / trash bags | as needed | ☐ | Easy to forget — trash bags especially (courtside bins fill up). |
| Sunscreen (shared bottle) | 1–2 | ☐ | Optional but appreciated; full sun both afternoons. |

Rough consumables budget: **$150–250**/year depending on field size and sales.

## 4. Next-year prep timeline

- **~4 weeks out:** confirm venue/date; open registration; line up trophy +
  shirt vendors (reuse last year's — see filled-in vendor columns above).
- **~1–2 weeks out:** close registration (leave a buffer); pull shirt size
  counts from the Merch tab and **place the tee + trophy orders**; confirm
  expected arrival dates in the tables above.
- **Event week:** verify orders **arrived** (flip the Confirmed boxes); buy
  non-perishables; borrow coolers.
- **Day before:** buy perishables + ice; charge the ops phones/laptop; set the
  cash-drawer starting float (Payments → Cash drawer).
- **After the event:** update the vendor/cost columns and quantities in this
  file with what you actually used, so next year starts from real numbers.
